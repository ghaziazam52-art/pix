01 / About

Built to explore what comes next.

Pixel Forge AI is an evolving, student-built learning workspace. It explores what an interface for AI-assisted learning could feel like, and experiments with existing language models along the way.

We are not claiming a trained foundation model or a finished product. This is a learning project, and we are building it in the open, one honest step at a time.

  • LearnExploring AI-assisted learning.
  • BuildDeveloping an accessible interface.
  • ExperimentTesting ideas and existing AI technologies.
